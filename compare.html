What changes for customers

  • One way to orderToday the page has three stacked headers, two QR codes and a "Book Your Table" button for a pickup-only restaurant. The concept has one Order Now button, always visible, wired to the existing ChowNow store.
  • Menu above the foldPrices currently live in a flyer image and a PDF. The concept puts every item and price in real text, so it shows up in Google and reads on a phone.
  • Real food photos, used wellSame photos Pinz already owns, cropped consistently in the nine-item grid instead of a wall of thumbnails.
  • The 4–6 PM deal sells itselfThe 10% off promo moves from a yellow ticker to a dedicated bar under the hero with its own order button.
  • Catering gets a page that convertsPackage prices, how it works in three steps, and a quote form. Today it is a page of paragraphs.
  • Consistent hoursThe current site says 11am–8pm in the header and 11am–6pm in the footer. The concept shows one set of hours everywhere.
  • Loads fastNo page builder, no 40-font stylesheet. Three static pages and one CSS file.
  • Built on Pinz's own brandMaroon and flame orange from the existing logo, so nothing needs to be reprinted.
