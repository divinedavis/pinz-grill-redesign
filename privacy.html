Privacy Policy

Pinz Grill iOS app and website · Last updated: September 27, 2026

The Pinz Grill app for iPhone shows the restaurant's menu, hours, deals and contact details, and hands you off to the restaurant's online ordering checkout when you want to place an order. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The website section below covers this website (divinedavis.com/pinz-grill/), including the catering request form and the map.

The app collects no personal data

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or notifications. Nothing you do in the app is recorded or sent to the developer.

Menu and hours are fetched from ChowNow

When the app opens it downloads the current menu, prices, hours, delivery fee and deals from ChowNow, the online-ordering service Pinz Grill already uses on pinzgrill.com. That request carries no identifying information other than what any internet request carries (your device's IP address), and the app keeps a copy of the last menu on your phone so it works offline. Menu item photos load from ChowNow's servers the same way.

Ordering happens on ChowNow, not in the app

Tapping Pickup or Delivery opens Pinz Grill's ChowNow checkout in a secure Safari window inside the app. Everything you enter there, including your name, phone number, delivery address, payment card and order history, is collected and processed by ChowNow under ChowNow's privacy policy, and by the restaurant to prepare your order. The app never sees it. Apple Pay, if you use it, is handled by Apple.

Links to other apps

Directions opens Apple Maps or Google Maps, Call opens the Phone app, and the Facebook and TikTok links open those services. Each is governed by its own privacy policy once you leave the app.

This website

The website has no accounts, no cookies of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels.

  • Catering request form. The Request a Quote form on the catering page does not send anything to a server. Pressing Send Request opens your own email app with a message to info@pinzgrill.com containing what you typed (name, phone, event date, guest count and your order). Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. The email then goes to the restaurant, which uses it to quote and prepare your catering order and keeps it in its inbox for as long as it needs it for that order.
  • Embedded Google Map. The home page shows an embedded map from Google Maps. When it loads, Google receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy. The Directions and address links open Google Maps.
  • Google Fonts. Page typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Hosting and server logs. The site is hosted on a DigitalOcean server. Like any web server it logs your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser type; these logs are used only for security and troubleshooting and are rotated out after a short period.
  • Links out. Order Online goes to Pinz Grill's ChowNow ordering page (see above), and the Facebook, TikTok and Instagram links go to those services. Each has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Neither the app nor the website sells or shares personal information or uses it for advertising. You can ask what information we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing the contacts below; we answer within 30 days (45 days where California law allows). California residents (CCPA/CPRA) have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and will not be treated differently for using these rights. EEA and UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR) may access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of their data, receive a portable copy, and complain to their data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). A catering request emailed to the restaurant is handled on the basis of taking the steps you asked for before an order; deletion requests for it go to info@pinzgrill.com. Orders placed through ChowNow are governed by ChowNow's policy.

Children

The app and website are not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK). The app collects no information from anyone; if a child has sent a catering request by email, the restaurant will delete it on request.

Changes

If a future version of the app or website starts collecting any data, this page (and, for the app, the App Store privacy label) will be updated before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy, the app or the website: divinejdavis@gmail.com (developer). Questions about an order or about the restaurant: info@pinzgrill.com or (839) 329-9352.
