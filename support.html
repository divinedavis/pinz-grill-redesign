App Support

Pinz Grill for iPhone

The Pinz Grill app puts the full menu on your phone and starts a pickup or delivery order in two taps. If something isn't working, the answers below cover the common questions.

How do I place an order?

Tap Pickup or Delivery on the Home tab, the Order tab, or any menu item. Pinz Grill's secure checkout opens inside the app: pick your items and sauces, pay with a card or Apple Pay, and the order goes straight to the kitchen. Tap Done in the top corner to come back to the app.

Is delivery available?

Yes, within the delivery area shown at checkout. The Order tab shows today's delivery hours, the delivery fee and the minimum order. Delivery is handled by the restaurant's courier partners, not by a marketplace app.

Where do I see past orders or my account?

Order tab → Past orders & account. Orders, saved cards and rewards live in the restaurant's ChowNow checkout account, so sign in there with the email you used to order.

The price in the app is different from the board in the store

The app shows online-ordering prices, which can differ slightly from the printed menu. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

The menu says "Saved copy"

The app could not reach the ordering system, so it is showing the last menu it downloaded. Check your connection and pull down on the Menu tab to refresh. Ordering needs a connection.

Catering

Call the restaurant at (839) 329-9352 during open hours, or email info@pinzgrill.com. The restaurant is at 3601 Broad River Road, Columbia, SC 29210.

Email divinejdavis@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what you tapped. Replies usually come within a day.
